Proposed build — the synthesis

Grouped by priority. Everything slots into the existing CloudNivo app; nothing gets rebuilt.

1 · Landing hero upgrade P1

Replace the static hero with the category-standard pattern: link-paste input as the primary action, a second conversational input ("describe what you want AI to make"), and example-prompt chips under both. Stat-strip with our real numbers.

2 · Gen page prompt chips P1

Vizard-style one-click chips: each viral format becomes a chip that fills the prompt + aspect ratio instantly. Adds "1 min ≈ 5 clips" style helper copy.

3 · Clip metadata: virality + hashtags P1

Klap-style potential score on finished generations, plus auto-generated description + hashtags (OpenAI, already wired) attached to every result.

4 · Stats-trio + use-case rail P2

Submagic's closing pattern: "10x faster · +40% views · 80% cheaper" before the final CTA, and a segmented "who we help" rail (creators / marketers / agencies).

5 · Prompt Library on landing P2

Public teaser of the seeded viral prompt library with one-click "try in studio" — pulls the EveryGen playbook onto the marketing surface.

6 · Brand template on clips P3

Feed the existing Brand Kit (fonts, colors, logo) into generated clip captions. Social scheduling stays out of scope for now — noted, not built.
